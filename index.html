Compass Critters

Tap a tile to reveal it. Empty is always safe and points toward the nearest critter. A critter costs a life — 3 per round.

A red arrow or ✦ means that critter is within 2 tiles.

✦ means two or more critters are exactly tied for nearest, in different directions. It's deliberately vague on its own — cross-reference a neighboring tile to narrow it down, or spend a Decode to see it directly.

Hold or right-click a tile to flag it as a reminder — optional, free, never required.

Win by revealing every tile that isn't a critter.

Power-ups

Sniff · 2 🪙 — reveals one still-hidden critter directly. No life lost.

Rewind · 2 🪙 — undoes your last tap: the tile, your life count, and your progress are all restored.

Pulse · 3 🪙 (5×5 boards and up) — tap any tile to learn how many critters sit in the 3×3 area around it, immune to masking.

Decode · 3 🪙 — tap a tile you've already revealed that shows ✦ to see exactly which directions are tied.

Ward · 2 🪙 — arm it to cover your very next tap: if it's a critter, no life lost. If it's safe, the ward is just spent.

Shield · 1 🪙 — arm it to protect your streak through your next round reset.
